Shikaku — Divide by Box

How to Play

Every puzzle starts the same way: a grid dotted with numbers. Draw a shape around each number so its area matches that number exactly — no more, no less. Do that for every clue, cover every single cell, and the puzzle is solved.

  • 01 One shape, one number: never two clues, never zero.
  • 02 Exact area equality: total cells must match the number precisely.
  • 03 100% tessellation: shapes cannot overlap or leave any gaps.

Weird Mode

Same exact rule, zero straight-edge constraints. Shapes bend, branch, and tessellate into freeform polyominoes (L-shapes, T-shapes, Crosses, Serpents). Features 1,000 dedicated polyomino campaign puzzles.

Three Play Styles

Structured progression or endless procedural puzzles.

Campaign Progression

2,000 handcrafted levels (1,000 Classic + 1,000 Weird) spanning 26 dimensions, from starter 5×5 grids to 30×30 master challenges.

Random Play

Four curated difficulty tiers — Easy, Medium, Hard, and Expert — procedural CSP generation delivering a unique, verified solvable puzzle every time.

Custom Grid Sandbox

Choose any width and height from 5×5 up to 30×30 to create your own customized spatial challenge.

Mastery & 3-Star Challenge

Every level systematically tracks three mastery conditions.

★

Star 1: Level Clear

Partition the whole board completely with zero empty cells and zero overlaps.

★★

Star 2: Speed Master

Complete the puzzle inside the targeted par time established for that specific grid dimension.

★★★

Star 3: Flawless Finish

Solve within par moves with 0 hints, 0 undos/redos, and 0 erases. Real-time feedback tags clearly inform you if a tool was used.

Earn a prestigious Milestone Crown every 50 solved campaign levels in either mode — celebrating steady mastery, not just a lucky solve.
