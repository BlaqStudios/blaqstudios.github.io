Flux Wall
Endless Arcade Action

One thumb. One tap. A shaft that never stops falling away beneath you, and every 25 points the rules of physics themselves flux — gravity flips heavy, floors turn to ice, the world inverts. Flux Wall —is an endless reflex arcade game where the only thing that stays constant is that dying is always your fault, never the game’s.

How It Works

Simple controls, deep mastery.

The Core Loop

Tap anywhere to hop — part upward, part sideways — sending the ball on a parabolic arc toward one of two walls. Direction alternates automatically: tap 1 goes left, tap 2 right, tap 3 left, and so on. Land on a safe wall segment to score +1 and reflect back into open space. Hit an obstacle, the top, or the bottom — run ends.

Skills That Scale

Three independent difficulty axes rise together as your score climbs: reaction speed tightens, the safe target shrinks, and obstacles gain movement — but thanks to the fairness invariant, a reachable gap always exists on each wall’s own track. Fair by construction.
