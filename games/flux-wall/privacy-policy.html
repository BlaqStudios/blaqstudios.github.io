Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

At Blaq Studios, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through our compliance with this policy.

This policy describes the types of information we may collect from you when you use Flux Wall and our practices for collecting, using, maintaining, protecting, and disclosing that information.

Information We Collect

Flux Wall may collect certain information automatically, including but not limited to:

  • Device information (model, operating system version)
  • Usage data (how you interact with the game)
  • Advertising identifiers (for ad-supported versions)

How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our game
  • Deliver advertising (in ad-supported versions)
  • Analyze trends and usage patterns
  • Communicate with you about updates and promotions

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We may share your information with:

  • Service providers who assist us in operating our game
  • Advertising partners (to serve relevant ads)
  • Analytics providers
  • As required by law or to protect our legal rights

Your Choices

You can opt out of certain data uses by adjusting your device settings or through in-game options where available.

Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of your data.

Children's Privacy

Flux Wall is designed for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.
